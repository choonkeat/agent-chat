> **One change ships next: agent-chat stops repeating its standing instructions to the AI.** (Released version is 0.13.0; this would be 0.13.1 or 0.14.0.)
>
> What it does, in plain terms:
>
> 1. **Instructions sent less often.** Every time you send a message, agent-chat used to attach the same long block of "how to reply" instructions. Now the full block goes out every 10th message; the ones in between get a single-line reminder.
> 2. **Shorter "got it" reply.** When the AI posts a status update, agent-chat used to answer with a reminder paragraph. Now it answers "ok".
> 3. **Your style note sent once per batch.** If you send several messages before the AI reads them, your writing-style note (the "explain in plain language, reply for ADHD" paragraph) used to be copied in front of each message. Now it goes once, above the whole group.
>
> Why it matters: the AI has a limit on how much text it can hold at once (its "context"). Measured across 89 of your past sessions, two-thirds of what agent-chat sent the AI was these repeats. Removing them cuts agent-chat's total text by 28%, so the AI has more room for your actual work.
>
> What you'll notice: nothing visible in the chat window. It's behind-the-scenes.
>
> The other 2 commits since 0.13.0 are saved chat logs and a write-up of the measurement — they don't change the published package.
